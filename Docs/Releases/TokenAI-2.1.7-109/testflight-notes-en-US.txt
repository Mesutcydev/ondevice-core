Model management and languages, on top of 2.1.7 (108):
• French, Portuguese, Italian, Greek, Romanian, Russian, Simplified Chinese and Korean now have translations for the Models, Settings, chat and Lens screens (about a quarter of the strings were translated before). Screens built directly in the app, such as Storage Cleanup, the coding workspace and Diagnostics, are still in English in this build.
• Image-model downloads can be cancelled from Models, Downloads and Image studio.
• Storage Cleanup lists image, Core AI and classifier models too, and leaves an image model alone while it is still downloading.
• Downloads show how much has transferred and roughly how long is left.
• A gated model that needs a Hugging Face token: Resume opens the token sheet, then retries.
• Model search keeps "Search Hugging Face" available while you type; Import shows that it is working.
• Deleting the Lens model that is loaded now frees its memory.

Known in this build: Greek and Russian sidebar names such as "Στούντιο εικόνων" are cut off. In Storage Cleanup, "Partial downloads" starts ticked and clearing it restarts paused downloads, and the result message can say "Storage cleaned" when a model could not be removed.

Please try: switch the app language to one of the eight and look through Models, Settings and chat; start an image-model download and cancel it; open Storage Cleanup (untick Partial downloads if you have paused downloads); watch the time-left label on a model download.
